# week8

I created a webcam that makes two different effects depending on the color sticky note shown on screen. I have very dark brown eyes (almost black) and I wanted to accentuate this by creating an effect where my eyes would stand out. I picked out only the darkest values (brightness under 35) to be affected, and everything else gets turned to white/black. 

When the blue stickynote is present, the screen turns black and the darkest shadows turn red. when the pink sticky note is present, the screen turns white and the darkest shadows turn blue. A cool side effect is that when both are present, the screen turns blue and the shadows turn white. Along with my eyes, parts of my hair and beard will also get turned red/blue depending on the sticky note shown. I particularly like most the way my eyes look with the effect, it makes them pop against the background, especially the red on black.
