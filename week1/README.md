## Week 1 Reading Response
As codes of all sorts have been used for thousands of years, determining what code is seems to have different meaning depending on the time period and context of the situation it is being used in. In my personal context, I would say that code is a way for me to communicate with a computer and describe what I want it to do.

I have very little coding experience right now, but becoming better at this skill would enable a whole new frontier of possibilities for myself once I get the hang of it. I can see the benefits of using code to have full creative control of my work, rather than being limited by determined options in pre-built software. On the flip side of that, I could also see code working against me in terms of my efficiency. Building code from scratch to get an extra degree of customizability in what I’m working on may not not be worth the time in certain situations where a pre-built software could get me a similar result.

This semester I am excited in general to get more familiar with the syntax of coding. In my limited experience, this is something that I’ve found pretty difficult and intimidating to get the hang of. Knowing how to write what I want to write without having to refer to any guides will help me have more creative freedom and flow when I am coding.
